Privacy policy

This explains, in plain language, what Fitness Coach collects, why, who else touches it, and how to delete it. Fitness Coach is a small personal project, not a company. It has no ads, no analytics or tracking scripts, and doesn't sell or rent your data.

What we collect

  • Account: username, password (stored only as a one-way hash), email address, and date of birth.
  • About you: the answers you give in the About-you questions (for example goals, experience, and preferences).
  • Body data: weight entries, body measurements, and progress photos you upload.
  • Activity from your phone (optional): steps, active calories, distance and workouts, only if you connect Health Connect (see below).
  • Logs: food and calorie logs, custom foods and recipes, workouts, routines, rest days, and goals.
  • Social (optional, adults and linked families only): your profile, who you share with, comments, blocks, and parent–teen links.
  • Technical: a session cookie that keeps you logged in (up to 14 days), and normal server logs from our host. On the phone app, reminder settings are kept on your device only.

Why we use it

Only to run the app: show your logs and progress, give recommendations, keep your account secure, apply the age rules below, and send account emails.

Email

Your email is used for account messages such as password resets. These are sent through Brevo, an email delivery service, which receives your address and the message content to deliver it. We don't send marketing email.

Who else handles your data

  • Render hosts the app server.
  • MongoDB Atlas stores the database, including progress photos.
  • Brevo delivers account emails.
  • Open Food Facts receives the barcode number when you scan or type a barcode (not who you are).

Age rules and teens

  • Fitness Coach is for people 13 and older. If a date of birth shows someone is under 13, no account is created, or an existing one is locked so it can only be deleted.
  • Teens (13–17) can't use search, public profiles, or sharing and comments with anyone except a parent they have linked. These limits lift automatically at 18.
  • A linked parent sees only what the teen chooses to share with them through the app's sharing settings.
  • Email and date of birth are never shown on profiles, in search, or in anything shared.

Health Connect (Android app)

If you choose to connect it in Settings → Connected health data, the Android app reads these from Health Connect:

  • steps
  • active calories burned
  • distance
  • exercise sessions (type, start and end time, duration, calories, distance, and the name of the app or device that recorded them)

Fitness Coach never writes to Health Connect.

  • How it's used: only for features you see in the app. That means daily step and activity totals on your dashboard, imported workouts in your workout history, and your training streak. Active calories are shown for information only and don't change your calorie target.
  • Storage: the last 30 days are copied to Fitness Coach's servers as daily totals and workout records so they show up on all your devices. They're kept with the rest of your account data.
  • Not sold or used for ads: data from Health Connect is never sold or used for advertising. It isn't used for credit, insurance or similar decisions, and isn't shared with anyone except as described here.
  • Teens: for a teen account with a linked parent who can already see habit information, the parent may also see the teen's daily step counts and imported workouts. Nothing else from Health Connect is shared.
  • Your control: you can disconnect at any time in the app or in Health Connect's own settings. "Remove imported health data" in Settings deletes everything imported. Deleting your account deletes it too.
  • Google's rules: Fitness Coach's use of information received from Health Connect follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Camera and notifications (phone app)

The camera is used only while the barcode scanner is open, to read the barcode. Video isn't recorded or uploaded. Workout and streak reminders are scheduled on your phone; we don't use push notification servers.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Account → Delete account. This removes your account and everything linked to it, including logs, weights, measurements, photos, About-you answers, recipes, custom foods, profile, shares, comments, and parent–teen links. Deletion happens right away in the live database. It also signs you out on all devices. Backups: the database runs on MongoDB Atlas's free tier, which doesn't have automatic cloud backups turned on, so deleted data isn't kept in scheduled database backups. Occasional manual backups made for maintenance could briefly contain some of your data until they're removed.

Security

Traffic is encrypted (HTTPS), passwords are hashed, and sign-in cookies can't be read by scripts. No system is perfectly secure.
