        Show on my profile

        Share access

        Give a coach or family member read-only access to the parts of your log you choose. You can remove it anytime.

        What they can see

        Training schedule

        Your streak counts training days in a row. Rest days in your schedule never break it.

        Training days (optional)

        Pick weekdays for a fixed schedule. Leave all off to use a number of rest days a week instead.

        Flex / holiday days

        Cap how many flex days you can mark each month. Estimates learn from your weigh-ins over the next few days. Adults can optionally spread a small calorie buffer to the days around a flex day (off by default). Members under 18 never see deficit or compensation advice.

        When on, about 25% of the estimated surplus is split across the day before and after.

        Recurring practice days

        Optional. Practice days work with your streak like scheduled training — log a sport session on those days to keep the streak.

        Privacy policy · Terms of use

        Delete account

        This permanently deletes your account and everything in it: food and workout logs, weights, measurements, progress photos, About-you answers, goals, routines, recipes, custom foods, your profile, shares, comments, and any parent–teen links. It can't be undone.

        Connected health data

        Read steps, active calories, distance and workouts from Health Connect (your watch or fitness apps sync there). Fitness Coach only reads. It never writes to Health Connect, and the data is used only for features in this app.

          Remove imported health data

          Deletes every step count, calorie total and workout imported from your phone from Fitness Coach's servers. Your data in Health Connect isn't touched.

          Reminders

          Notifications from this phone. They're scheduled on the device, so they work even when the server is asleep.

          Scan barcode

          Point the camera at a barcode. On a computer, type the number.
